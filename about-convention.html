About the Convention

The National Scientific Convention on Homoeopathy is envisioned as a significant national-level initiative to advance the science, practice, education and research of Homoeopathy by bringing together eminent Homoeopathic physicians, academicians, researchers, scientists, healthcare professionals, policymakers, postgraduate scholars, interns, practitioners and students from across the country.

The convention will serve as a dynamic platform for the presentation and exchange of evidence-based scientific findings, clinical experiences, innovative research outcomes and contemporary developments in Homoeopathic healthcare, thereby fostering meaningful interdisciplinary interaction and bridging traditional medical practices with modern scientific approaches.

It will provide an essential opportunity to deliberate on national healthcare priorities, align Homoeopathic education and clinical practice with evolving national health goals, and discuss regulatory, academic and professional standards prescribed by apex bodies such as the National Commission for Homoeopathy (NCH) and the Central Council for Research in Homoeopathy (CCRH).

Furthermore, the convention will encourage rigorous clinical research, promote a strong research culture among students and young professionals, facilitate academic collaboration among institutions, and enhance professional development through knowledge exchange, scientific deliberations and clinical learning on complex health conditions, emerging therapeutic approaches and contemporary challenges in Homoeopathic medical education and healthcare.

By creating an environment conducive to scientific inquiry, evidence-based practice and constructive dialogue, the convention will contribute towards strengthening academic excellence, improving the quality of healthcare delivery, elevating public trust and scientific credibility, and enhancing the national visibility of Homoeopathy as an integral component of the broader healthcare system.

THE 2026 EDITION

The proposed convention will be organised under the central theme:

“Homoeopathy in Contemporary Healthcare: Evidence, Innovation, Research and Integrative Approaches for Emerging Health Challenges.”

The National Scientific Convention on Homoeopathy will focus on the following scientific themes:

1. Evidence-Based Homoeopathy for Non-Communicable Diseases (NCDs)

Exploring the clinical role, research evidence and scope of Homoeopathy in the comprehensive management of chronic diseases, including diabetes, hypertension, cardiovascular diseases and other non-communicable conditions.

2. Homoeopathy for Wellness, Preventive Healthcare & Healthy Ageing

Exploring health promotion, preventive healthcare, quality of life and healthy ageing and the role of Homoeopathy within a comprehensive, patient-centred healthcare framework.

3. Homoeopathy in Emergency Medicine, Trauma Care

Discussing emergency preparedness, first aid, Basic Life Support (BLS), Advanced Life Support (ALS), trauma management and the role of Homoeopathic institutions in emergency response, alongside evidence-based conventional emergency treatment.

4. Palliative Treatment, Management of Side Effects of Cancer Medicines & Terminal Cancer Care

Exploring research into supportive care, symptom management, quality of life and integrative approaches for patients undergoing cancer treatment, without replacing established oncological therapies.

5. Homoeopathy in Veterinary Healthcare

Exploring scientific research and clinical evidence on the application of Homoeopathy in animal healthcare, disease management, animal welfare and veterinary practice, with emphasis on research methodology, treatment safety and evidence-based approaches to animal health.

6. Homoeopathy in Autoimmune, Allergic & Immunological Disorders

Exploring scientific research, clinical evidence, immunological mechanisms and multidisciplinary approaches to autoimmune diseases, allergic conditions and immune-mediated disorders.

7. Homoeopathic Drug Research, Standardisation & New Drug Development

Focusing on drug standardisation, pharmacopoeial standards, quality control, pharmacological research, clinical trials, research methodology and the development of scientifically validated Homoeopathic medicines.

8. Homoeopathy in Public Health, Community Medicine & Integrative Healthcare

Exploring community health programmes, rural and urban healthcare delivery, maternal and child health, public-health research, multidisciplinary collaboration and the role of Homoeopathic institutions in strengthening healthcare services.

Additional Focus Areas for Scientific Sessions

  • Homoeopathy in Maternal & Child Healthcare
  • Approaches to Common Chronic Diseases, including chronic respiratory diseases, neurological disorders and digestive disorders.
  • Homoeopathy in Medical Education
